Since 1980

A Legacy Rooted in Jodia Bazaar

Royal Rice was founded in 1980 in Jodia Bazaar — Karachi's oldest and most respected grain, spice and grocery market — where the finest Basmati, Sella and Brown rice have long been sourced, graded and traded by hand.

What started as a single storefront built on honest weight and careful grain selection has grown into a trusted name across both retail households and restaurant kitchens, without ever leaving the street where it began.

MH
Owner & Proprietor

Musab Hamdani

Musab Hamdani continues the family's founding philosophy — that quality rice starts with patient grading and honest sourcing, not shortcuts. Based at the original Jodia Bazaar location, he oversees every relationship, from the family buying a monthly sack of Basmati to the restaurant ordering by the ton.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

1980

Founded in Jodia Bazaar

Royal Rice opens its doors as a single grain stall, specialising in hand-graded Basmati rice for Karachi households.

1990s

Expanding the Grain Range

Sella, Brown and White Basmati rice are added, alongside relationships with restaurants across the city.

2000s

Entering Restaurant Supply

Royal Rice grows into a full restaurant supplier — sauces, noodles & pastas, flours, oils, masala and dry ingredients.

Today

Retail & Wholesale, Under One Roof

Musab Hamdani leads Royal Rice's next chapter — serving families and businesses alike with the same graded quality since 1980.
