Our Story

Born from a passion for purity and a commitment to bringing the finest grains of Pakistan to every home.

Rooted in Tradition, Driven by Quality

Aqaab Rice was founded with a single mission: to deliver the purest, most fragrant basmati and specialty rice directly to Pakistani families. The name "Aqaab" — meaning Eagle in Urdu — symbolizes our sharp eye for quality and precision.

We work with experienced rice cultivators across Pakistan's most fertile regions, ensuring that every grain meets our strict standards before it reaches your table.

From our signature 1121 Basmati to our nutritious Brown Rice and aromatic Chai Patti, each product line represents our dedication to bringing you the best nature has to offer.

  • Hygienic packaging to preserve freshness
